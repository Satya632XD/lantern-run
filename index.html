Guide to Whispering Pines

You are stranded in a dark, shifting forest. Reach the Forest Gate before your lantern burns out or the creatures find you.

🏮 Lantern Light

Each step or turn costs 1 fuel. Low fuel shrinks your sight radius. If fuel reaches 0%, darkness takes you.

✨ Fireflies

Gather glowing firefly swarms along paths to restore +25 lantern fuel and keep the shadows at bay.

🏛️ Ancient Shrines

Step into stone shrines to unveil an expansive area of the forest and restore 1 Lantern Flare charge.

🌿 Misty Groves

Dense thickets camouflage you from prowling beasts. Hide inside them to break line of sight!

🐾 Shadow Stalkers

Dangerous beasts roam the woods. When nearby, they track your steps. Evade them or use a Flare.

⚡ Blinding Flare

Press [F] or tap Flare to burst radiant light, stunning all nearby creatures for 3 turns (costs 2 fuel).

Controls:
  • Arrow Keys or WASD: Move
  • Space: Wait (Pass 1 turn)
  • F: Lantern Flare (Stun)
  • P or Esc: Pause
  • R: Restart Run
  • M: Mute Sound
